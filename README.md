SafetyStory AI 🦺

AI-powered safety storytelling and HSE learning platform

SafetyStory AI transforms workplace safety scenarios into engaging, structured stories designed to improve hazard awareness, safety communication, and learning.

The application combines AI creative ideation, scenario-based storytelling, and HSE-focused messaging to turn everyday workplace hazards into memorable learning experiences.



🎯 Purpose

Traditional safety communication can become repetitive and difficult for workers to remember.

SafetyStory AI takes a different approach:

«Turn safety lessons into stories people remember.»

Users provide a workplace safety scenario, and the application generates multiple creative approaches before allowing the user to develop the selected concept into a complete safety story.



✨ Key Features

Creative Ideation

Generates multiple creative campaign concepts from a single safety scenario.

Possible approaches include:

- 🎬 Cinematic
- 🎭 Dramatic
- 🎥 Documentary
- 🎮 Interactive
- 📖 Storytelling
- 🚀 Futuristic
- 📰 Incident/Report-style concepts

The available approaches may vary depending on the scenario.

Structured Safety Stories

Generated stories follow a consistent narrative structure:

1. Inciting Incident
2. Conflict
3. Turning Point
4. Resolution
5. Golden Takeaway

This structure helps transform technical safety information into an engaging learning experience.

HSE-Focused Messaging

Stories emphasize practical safety principles including:

- Hazard identification
- Risk awareness
- Safe systems of work
- PPE
- Work at height
- MEWP safety
- Dropped-object prevention
- Exclusion zones
- Permit-to-work considerations
- Stop-work authority
- Procedural compliance

Creative Quality Evaluation

SafetyStory AI evaluates generated content using creative and learning-oriented indicators such as:

- Memorability
- Resonance
- Clarity
- Safety Impact

These indicators are intended as AI-generated quality assessments, not formal safety certifications or statistical measurements.

🏗️ Example Scenario

Input

A worker is performing work at height from a MEWP at approximately 8 meters. The worker's lanyard is attached to an unsuitable anchorage point. Loose tools are present in the MEWP basket. Workers are present below the MEWP. No injury has occurred.

Example Generated Concepts

Cinematic — The Unseen Fall

«"One loose tool. One wrong attachment. One second too late."»

Interactive — Your Call: 8 Meters Up

«"Every choice you make at height could be the one that changes everything."»

Documentary — Anatomy of a Near Miss: The 8-Meter Warning

«"It wasn't an accident. It was a warning."»

Dramatic — The Cost of 'Just This Once'

«"He knew the rules. So did she. But complacency whispers a dangerous lie."»



⚠️ Responsible AI & HSE Disclaimer

SafetyStory AI is an AI-assisted educational and creative tool.

Generated stories may contain fictional characters, dialogue, narrative elements, or illustrative events depending on the selected storytelling mode.

AI-generated content must not be treated as an official incident investigation, legal interpretation, engineering determination, risk assessment, method statement, permit, or regulatory certification.

Before publication or workplace use, users should review technical and regulatory information against:

- Applicable legislation
- Regulatory requirements
- Company procedures
- Client requirements
- Manufacturer instructions
- Applicable standards
- Site-specific risk assessments and controls

For actual incidents or near misses, factual incident information should always come from the appropriate site records and investigation process.

🧪 Testing

The application is being evaluated using controlled safety scenarios.

Current testing focuses on whether the system can:

- Preserve the original safety scenario
- Generate distinct creative concepts
- Maintain HSE relevance
- Avoid unnecessary fabrication
- Distinguish hazards from actual incidents
- Produce useful safety takeaways
- Maintain creative engagement

Testing results are used to refine the application incrementally.

🤝 Contributing

Contributions, ideas, testing feedback, and suggestions are welcome.

2. Explain the expected behavior.

4. Include relevant screenshots or logs where appropriate.

Do not enter confidential, personally identifiable, commercially sensitive, or restricted workplace information unless the application is specifically configured and authorized to handle such data.
